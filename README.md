# Cradle to Cosmos

A turn-based nation-building strategy game for phones and computers. Lead your people from a Stone Age village to a colony on Mars, on a map of the real world.

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or install needed. Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## The game
- **193 real-world regions** with terrain and resources (horses, iron, oil, spices, silk and more).
- **8 ages**, 46 technologies: Stone, Bronze, Classical, Medieval, Renaissance, Industrial, Modern and Information.
- **Your nation runs itself.** Population growth, food sharing, trade and resource imports happen automatically. You steer with buildings (31 types), laws and diplomacy.
- **Politics:** 9 governments, 7 laws, and 7 factions whose approval drives stability. Expect unrest, revolts, coups, elections and 35 random events.
- **War & diplomacy:** 15 AI rival nations with personalities. Trade pacts, alliances, gifts, tribute, war and peace.
- **Win** by founding a Mars colony, ruling 40% of the world, or leading in score in 2100.

## Files
- `index.html`: page and styles
- `game.js`: game logic, AI and UI
- `world.js`: generated world map (region shapes, neighbors, sea routes) built from Natural Earth land data via world-atlas
